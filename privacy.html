TradeMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

TradeMind is a private trading journal. This page explains what information the app handles and what it does with it. In short: your journal is yours, it is used only to run the app for you, and it is never sold or shared for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name (if you give one) and your password, handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase). Passwords are never visible to us in plain text.
  • Journal content you enter: trades, emotional check-ins, daily reviews, life journal entries, trading plans, settings, screenshots, and screen recordings or voice notes you choose to add.

How it is stored

  • Your journal is saved on your own device first, and then synced to a private account in Supabase (database and file storage). Access rules ensure each account can only read and change its own data.
  • If you choose the Google Drive option for recordings, those files are saved to a folder named "TradeMind" in your own Google Drive.

Google Drive and Google user data

  • The Google Drive option is optional. It asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets TradeMind see and manage only the files and folders that TradeMind itself created. It cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • TradeMind uses this permission only to upload, play back and delete the recordings you add. The Google sign-in token is kept in your browser's memory for the current session and is not stored on our servers.
  • TradeMind's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can disconnect at any time from Settings, and remove the app's access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party access.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your journal or Google data for advertising or to train AI models.
  • We do not share your data with other users or third parties, other than the service providers that run the app (Supabase for storage, Google for the optional Drive feature, and GitHub for hosting the website).
  • The built-in coach works on your own data inside your browser using fixed rules. It does not send your journal to an AI service.

Your control

  • You can edit or delete any entry, recording or screenshot in the app.
  • You can download a full backup of your journal from Settings.
  • Logging out removes the local copy from that device after syncing.
  • To have your whole account and its stored data deleted, contact us using the address below.

Security

Connections use HTTPS and each account's data is protected by row-level security. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep important backups.

Not financial advice

TradeMind is a journaling tool. It does not provide financial advice and is not a broker or trading platform.
