TradeMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

TradeMind is a private online trading journal available at https://trevqioagency.github.io/trademind/ (the "app"). It lets traders record trades, follow their own trading plan, track emotions and daily habits, and review their results. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, where it is stored, who can see it, and how you can delete it. In short: your journal belongs to you, it is used only to run the app for you, and it is never sold or used for advertising.

1. Who is responsible for the app

TradeMind is developed and operated by its publisher, contactable at emochachabunda@gmail.com. Use this address for any privacy question or request.

2. Information the app handles

  • Account information: your email address, an optional display name, and your password. Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth) and are stored only in hashed form; we cannot read them.
  • Journal content you create: trades (instruments, prices, results, notes), your trading plan and rules, settings, daily and weekly checks and reviews, emotional check-ins, life-journal entries (such as sleep, stress and mood), and any screenshots, screen recordings or voice notes you choose to add.
  • Technical data stored on your device: a local copy of your journal (so the app works offline), your sign-in session, and a few small settings, kept in your browser's storage. The app does not use advertising or tracking cookies and has no third-party analytics.

3. How we use the information

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in on any device.
  • To save your journal and sync it between your devices.
  • To calculate your statistics, dashboards, reviews and rule-based coaching, which run inside your own browser.
  • To send account emails you request, such as email confirmation and password reset.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or to train artificial-intelligence models.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data, do not share it with other users, and do not give it to advertisers. It is shared only with the service providers listed below that are needed to run the app, or where the law requires it.

5. Where your data is stored and who handles it

  • Your journal is saved on your device first, then synced to a private account in Supabase (database and file storage). Access rules (row-level security) allow each account to read and change only its own data.
  • The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Screenshots, recordings and voice notes are kept in your private Supabase cloud storage, and only your account can open them.
  • These providers process data only to run the service.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept for as long as your account exists, so that your journal stays available to you.
  • You can edit or delete any entry, screenshot or recording inside the app at any time. Deleted items are removed from your account storage.
  • Logging out removes the local copy of your journal from that device after syncing.
  • To delete your whole account and all data stored for it, email the contact address above from the email you registered with. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • You can download a full backup of your journal from Settings before deleting.

7. Security

Connections use HTTPS and each account's data is protected by row-level security. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your own backups of important records.

8. Children

TradeMind is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your information using the app, or by contacting us. If you are in a region with data-protection laws (for example the UK, EU or Nigeria), you may also have the right to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Not financial advice

TradeMind is a journaling tool. It is not a broker or trading platform and does not provide financial advice.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated, and for significant changes we will notify users in the app.

12. Contact

Questions, access or deletion requests: emochachabunda@gmail.com
