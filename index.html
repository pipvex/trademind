Method B tracker

Your official H4 → H1 → M15 entry model.

01

H4 zone

Identify a relevant H4 supply or demand zone.

02

H1 order block

Find an H1 order block inside or near the H4 zone.

03

M15 close beyond the boundary

Wait for an M15 candle to close beyond the relevant H1 order block boundary.

04

Next-candle break

Enter only when the immediately following M15 candle breaks the confirmation candle's high (buy) or low (sell).

05

Risk and execution
