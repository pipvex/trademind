TradeMind - a private trading journal

TradeMind is a free, private trading journal for forex, gold and index traders. It helps you record every trade, follow your own trading plan, review your emotions and habits, and see your results improve over time. Your journal is saved to your own private account, so you can log in on any phone or computer and find the same data.

What you can do with TradeMind

  • Write your own trading plan and run a Yes / No checklist before every trade.
  • Log trades with automatic risk, lot size, R multiple, profit / loss, balance and drawdown.
  • Add before / after screenshots, screen recordings and voice notes to each trade (stored in your private cloud).
  • Record how you feel before and after trades, plus a private daily life journal.
  • See your dashboard, daily and weekly reviews, and simple rule-based coaching from your own data.

Your privacy

Your journal is private to your account. It is saved on your device and synced to your own protected account in the cloud so you can use it on any device. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with other users. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

TradeMind is a journaling tool. It is not a broker, does not place trades and does not give financial advice.
