SMC / ICT Chart Analyzer

Works on your device with no internet and no AI. It reads your chart pictures and marks zones, structure and possible entries.
Study tool only. Do not use it as a trading signal. It can misread a chart and does not know the news, spreads or what price does next. Always check the chart yourself and never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
