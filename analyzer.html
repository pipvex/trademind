SMC / ICT Offline Analyzer

No AI and no internet. The code reads the candles from your screenshots, then applies fixed SMC/ICT rules: S/R, OB, BB, FVG, IFVG, inducement, BOS, CHoCH, liquidity, and a long/short plan with SL and TP from your 3-timeframe lesson. Educational tool, not financial advice.
Settings

Add your screenshots

Choose your 3 screenshots (or paste with Ctrl+V) and press Analyze. The app finds the chart area, the candle colors and the price scale by itself. Open the "Advanced" box under a picture only if something is read wrong. Tips for best results: show the price axis on the right, zoom so candles are at least 3px wide, and hide drawings on the chart.
